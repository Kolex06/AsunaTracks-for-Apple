# v1.0.0-beta.3 — Discover and continuation across Apple devices

App version 1.0, build 7 for Mac, iPhone/iPad, Apple Watch, and widgets.

- Continue Watching now uses compact cover cards with a smaller cover, two-line title, current progress, and a direct +1 action beside the artwork. Repeated status and next-episode rows are removed; accessibility text can still expand.
- Responsive continuation cards fit phone, tablet and Mac widths, with snapping and previous/next controls.
- Media episode pages add title/number search, All/Unwatched filters, clearer artwork fallbacks, watched checks and a guarded next-episode action.
- `webm-sound(...)` autoplays with sound without the extra play button; embedded videos use inline playback on iPhone/iPad.
- Watch groups anime and reading, shows clearer cover/title rows, and offers direct +1 actions in its list.
- Continue Reading separates manga/novels from anime; View all opens the corresponding active list.
- Quick progress actions check fresh account/list data, reject stale taps, preserve existing tracking fields, and update widgets after confirmation.
- For You shows the website’s personalized ranking, recommendation reasons, and genre mixes. Generic results are explicitly labeled Discover picks.
- Discover adds upcoming, most-tracked, highest-rated, recently updated, and character rails plus genre navigation.
- Media, character, and recommendation grids align cards to the top so different text lengths cannot shift neighboring covers.
- Includes the earlier shared community, animated artwork, role badge, and native API page improvements on iPhone/iPad and Mac.

Mac ZIP is development-signed and not notarized. iPhone/iPad IPA and Watch ZIP require the recipient’s own Apple signing. No TestFlight invitation is included.
