# v1.0.0-beta.2 — Community and interface update

Small beta update for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. App version 1.0, build 6.

- Native community post creation, comments, replies, and API-backed likes.
- Reply editors expand beneath individual comments; drafts survive closing the editor.
- Animated GIF/APNG avatars and banners, improved post media sizing, and smoother embedded-media scrolling on Mac.
- Updated role badges, including the faceted sponsor diamond.
- Cleaner glass Mac sidebar with the account centered at the bottom.
- More stable community loading and pagination.
- Native API pages for seasons, genres, and announcements.
- Source-build packaging fix and clearer Xcode project instructions.

## Downloads

- Mac: development-signed app ZIP; not notarized.
- iPhone and iPad: unsigned universal IPA, including the companion Watch app and widgets. Requires your own Apple signing.
- Apple Watch: unsigned Watch app ZIP. Requires your own Apple signing.
- Source: open AsunaTracks.xcodeproj with Xcode 27+. Requires OS 26+.

No TestFlight invitation is included. Account writes were tested with intercepted HTTP responses, without publishing test posts or comments to live accounts.
